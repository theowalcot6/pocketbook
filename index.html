Home Office and NPCC estimates put officer time on administration at around 40 percent. For a response officer that is three to four hours a day at a keyboard.

Statements written at 2am from memory lose detail. CPS and defence counsel find the gaps. Charges get dropped over missing descriptions and timings.

National File Standard failures cost weeks per case. Most are formatting and completeness errors rather than problems with the evidence itself.

Questions

What forces ask us.

Straight answers. If yours is not here, it is a good reason to book twenty minutes.

Is an AI-drafted statement admissible?

The statement is the officer's. Pocketbook produces a draft from the officer's own words, the officer reviews and signs it, and the officer gives evidence on it. That is the same position as a typed-up pocket notebook entry. We keep the audio and the edit history so the provenance question has a clear answer in disclosure.

Does this replace body-worn video?

No. BWV records the incident. Pocketbook records the officer's account of the incident afterwards, which is what a statement is. We do not transcribe BWV; speaker attribution in noisy footage is unreliable and we would rather not put that in front of a court.

Which record management systems do you integrate with?

We start by exporting into whatever your force uses. Direct integration into Niche, Connect and Athena is where our team's background is, and we will scope it with your IT lead during a pilot.

How does this fit with the national PoliceAI programme?

PoliceAI is prioritising digital evidence triage and disclosure at national scale. Pocketbook sits earlier in the process, at the point an officer gives their account. We are happy to be evaluated under any national framework and would rather be benchmarked than not.

What does a pilot look like?

One team, ten to twenty officers, six weeks. We measure minutes from incident to filed report, CPS return rate, and officer preference. No procurement beyond a data processing agreement and a DPIA, which we draft with your DPO.
