Pricing questions

The questions finance asks.

If the one you need is not here, it is a good reason to book twenty minutes.

Who counts as an officer for licensing?

Anyone who dictates. That is normally police constables and sergeants on response, neighbourhood and investigation teams. PCSOs and police staff investigators who take statements are licensed at the same rate. Supervisors who only review and sign are not charged.

Is the pilot really free? What is the catch?

It is free, and the catch is that we ask for your numbers. We want minutes from incident to filed report, CPS return rate and officer preference, measured properly, because they are how we improve the product and how you decide whether to continue. There is no obligation to convert, and we delete pilot data on your schedule if you do not.

Why per officer rather than per statement?

Because a per-statement price creates a reason not to write a statement, and that is the wrong incentive to put in front of an officer. A flat per-officer licence means the tenth witness account of the shift costs nothing extra.

What happens to the price after year one?

The per-officer rate agreed at signature is held for three years. Officer numbers can move up or down at each annual renewal. We do not apply inflation escalators inside the held period.

Which budget does this usually come from?

Most conversations so far have sat with the digital or ICT lead, with sponsorship from the head of response or criminal justice. The pilot is small enough to run from a local team budget. We are happy to write the business case alongside whoever owns it.

Are integrations extra?

Exporting into Niche, Connect and Athena is included in the Force tier. Two-way direct integration, where drafts appear inside the RMS and case data flows back, is a scoped piece of work priced separately for a single force and included for multi-force programmes.

Do you charge for the DPIA, data flow diagram or security evidence?

No. They are part of being a supplier to policing. The DPO pack is included at every tier, including the pilot.
